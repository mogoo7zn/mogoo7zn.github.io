01 / INTERESTS

Interests

Biological Sequence Modeling

Biological concepts and data are more scattered than mathematical and physical concepts, following statistical patterns. From data, we model biological sequences such as RNA and proteins, to help models understand statistical patterns, from physical principles, and design based on the understanding.

Reasoning for Science

Gene is the result of evolution and physical principles, and the sequence and function are just the manifestations of such result. Let the data speak itself, explaining the hidden logic behind it.
